MRMhub demo project
===================

Open Get-started.html for step-by-step instructions.

Contents
  MRMhub, mrmhub-viz    INTEGRATOR and its viewer for integration results
  MRMhub_plot.r         R script for chromatogram PDFs (INTEGRATOR step 4)
  param.txt, run_order.csv, feature_list.csv
                        INTEGRATOR inputs for the demo dataset
  mzML/                 499 targeted lipidomics LC-MS (MRM) analyses in
                        6 batches, in mzML format
  QUANT/                QUANT notebook (quant.qmd), metadata, and a
                        precomputed long.csv (QUANT/data/)
  Get-started.html      Instructions; also online at
                        https://slinghub.github.io/MRMhub/get-started.html
